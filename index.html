Guitar Synth

Play your acoustic guitar near the microphone and hear it as a clean electric guitar. Use headphones so the microphone doesn't hear the synth.

Calibrate (optional, hand in view): put your index finger on the nut → then on the 12th fret →

Runs entirely in your browser; no audio or video leaves your device. Guitar sound: FreePats "FSBS Electric Guitar Clean #1" (Creative Commons CC0). Detects single notes and common chords in standard tuning (E A D G B E).
